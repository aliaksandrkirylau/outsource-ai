# DECISIONS — decisions and assumptions in force

Every decision a human took about how to read the spec, what is in or out of
scope, or how the team works — with its date and source — so no later agent
reopens it. Append-only: a reversed decision is marked
`reversed by Dn, <date>` and a new entry is added.

Read this before drafting a ticket or "fixing" something that looks wrong.
