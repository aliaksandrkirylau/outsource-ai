# 2026-10-01 SOW v10.4 to v1 WBS scope mapping

Mikhail's working note, written from the KB at commit d06bc36. This is working material, not evidence.
Sources:

- [[2026-06-15 SOW v10.4 - rendering]], the latest SOW sent to the client.
- [[2026-09-25 v1 WBS - rendering]], tabs "Development Flutter" and "DevOps" only. The sheet was last
  modified on 2026-09-25 11:53 UTC, before the rendering was read on 2026-09-28, so the rendering is
  the current sheet.

Other WBS tabs (Discovery, the Flutter roll-up with QA, stabilization, PM and BA, and Assumptions &
Risks) are deliberately left out. A SOW item that lives in one of them is marked "other tab" below,
not "missing". There are no hours or money here.

References use the baseline format from `schema/types.md`: `WBS:<tab>!<row>`, and `SOW:M<n> <item>`
with (web) or (desktop) added where the SOW uses the same item name on both platforms.

Coverage: **full** means the WBI covers the SOW item as written. **partial** means the WBI exists but
part of the SOW wording has no estimate behind it. **none** means no WBI in the two tabs.

## Side findings worth a decision

- **Roles.** The SOW assumes "four primary user roles: Engineers, Managers, Supervisors, and
  Administrators". M2 and row 19 have three predefined roles ("Administrator, Engineer,
  Supervisor/Manager"). Either way, the SOW contradicts itself.
- **Audit volume.** "Up to 30 logged actions" appears only in the WBS. The SOW's "agreed volume" has
  no number, so under the contract's CR rules (COM-005) the cap is weak.
- **Sync.** This is the largest unestimated SOW item (1a #1). The revised WBS, an M1 deliverable, is
  the natural place to add it.
- **Inventory master data.** Low-stock levels and expiration dates (1a #4) need a decision on where
  they are entered. The SOW says the web admin panel, then rules out admin configuration beyond
  thresholds.
