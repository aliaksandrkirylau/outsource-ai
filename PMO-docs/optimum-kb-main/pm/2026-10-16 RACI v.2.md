# 2026-10-16 RACI v.2

The Optimum RACI, version 2 (Mikhail Andrushkevich, 16 October 2026). Working material, not evidence
(`pm/AGENTS.md`); internal only. No money.

Status: final. Agreed by the team and synced from the Google Sheet on 22 October 2026. Changes against
the 16 October version: the AM column became AM/SM, adding Anton Snotov (SM) next to Aliaksandr Kavalchuk,
on Aliaksandr's comment in the Sheet; and the note on Anton Oparienko as the main contact person was
added under the matrix. The assignments in rows 1 to 20 did not change.

Google Sheet copy: "Optimum RACI v.2"
(https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1E8vVNeWh68Z5G-Qt3T4ScpZ664mlI2UVzcE6bGL0BxU/edit, Mikhail's My Drive).

The split: the Lead PM is accountable for what we commit to and how we approach it; the PM is
accountable for running it.

A = accountable (one per row; accepts the result, cannot be delegated). R = does the work. C = must be
consulted, may stop the work if it is going wrong. I = must be informed. Per the PMO legend, C and I are
obligations, not courtesies. Until a TL is staffed, the DM covers the TL's column.
