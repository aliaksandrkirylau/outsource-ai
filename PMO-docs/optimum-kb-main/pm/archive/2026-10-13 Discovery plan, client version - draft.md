> **Discovery - how we propose to work**
>
> The goal of Milestone 1 is to agree, topic by topic, what the system does, in a form your engineers
> can click through, before we build it.
>
> How it works. For each topic we bring our first version, based on the agreed scope, and walk through
> it with your team in a session. Your team tells us what is missing or wrong; we revise it and send you
> the updated version to click through. Each topic is closed with your written confirmation by email,
> and we start building a topic once it is confirmed.
>
> Topics. Each covers the desktop application at the rig and the web application at HQ together:
> sign-in and roles; well jobs, mud checks and quality control; administration (users, thresholds,
> approvals, units); inventory; calculations and hydraulics; reports and approvals; dashboards. Plus one
> technical session on synchronisation, installing and updating the application on rig computers, and
> hosting.
>
> Schedule. Week 1: kickoff and an overview of the whole system. Weeks 2 to 4: sessions by topic. The
> Milestone 1 deliverables at the end of the month.
>
> Sessions. About ten in the month, two or three a week, 60 to 90 minutes, online, Monday to Thursday.
> From Innowise: our Business Analyst and Project Manager in every session, our Delivery Manager and
> architect in the technical one.
>
> What we need from you:
> - a project lead who can confirm each topic on your side;
> - one or two mud engineers and a field supervisor for the sessions, and an IT contact for the
>   technical session;
> - in the first week: the full set of formulas with the source manual; the mud-check parameter list per
>   mud system, with units and acceptable ranges; two or three filled daily reports and an end-of-section
>   and end-of-well report (anonymised); the product catalogue; the specification of the rig computers
>   and their connectivity; the KPIs you want on the dashboards.
>
> Result: the Milestone 1 deliverables listed in the SOW - Vision and Scope, Solution Architecture
> Design, UI Kit and the main mockups (as the confirmed clickable prototypes), communication plan,
> revised WBS and project plan.
