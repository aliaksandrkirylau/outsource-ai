# sources/contract/

- What: renderings of the signed contract and its confirmed annexes, and the contract chat. The original is in Drive; each overview links it. A draft, or a document not yet confirmed as an annex (an SOW version nobody has tied to the signed contract), goes to `sources/presale/` with a Question until it is confirmed.
- Trust: the signed text is mandatory. Deviating from it needs an agreement with the client. The contract chat is what people said while negotiating, not the text. Where language versions differ, check which the contract says prevails and ask a PM.
- Removed at ingest: every money amount, bank details, personal ID data (passport and ID numbers, home addresses), signatures and stamps. What stays is what creates a commitment or shapes delivery: obligations, dates, acceptance, change control, warranty, liability, IP, confidentiality, subcontracting, termination.
- Goes to the project notebook: yes, as cleaned here.
- Not for: amounts or rates (they stay in Drive and the P&L), or legal interpretation - ask a PM.
- Autoload: yes.
