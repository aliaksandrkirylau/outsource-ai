Mandatory once in force (`sources/contract/`). Innowise has signed it; the client has not yet, and the
Effective Date is not defined in the text. The Arabic text prevails in case of inconsistency (15.3);
only the English is rendered here, so anything that turns on wording needs the Arabic checked by a PM.

## Summary

A fixed-price, six-month delivery from an undefined Effective Date. Written acceptance only, within 14
business days per delivery, no deemed acceptance; all defects fixed at no charge; 12-month warranty
from final acceptance. Change requests only when signed by the client; clarifications and "omitted
functions already implicit in the SOW" are not change requests; Innowise confirms it had enough
information to scope and price. Payments on verified completion, month by month, the last on final
acceptance. Source code and IP pass to the client with each paid milestone. Full Saudi cybersecurity
and data protection compliance, nothing excluded. The client may terminate for convenience; Innowise
only for an undisputed payment default. Liability floor at the total price for ordinary claims, no cap
for the listed breaches. Remote work, no travel; minutes bind only when confirmed in writing; Saudi law,
arbitration in Riyadh. Clause 14.2 reads "within thirty (15 ) days" - the words and the figure differ.
