Hereinafter: “Second Party” or “Contractor”.

PREAMBLE Whereas the First Party operates in the oil and gas sector and wishes to develop a proprietary software system supporting oil- well drilling technology and maintenance; and whereas the Second Party represents that it possesses the experience, personnel and technical capability necessary to design, develop, test, deliver and support such software, the Parties agree as follows. The Preamble, Statement of Work (“SOW”), approved Financial Plan, and signed Change Requests form an integral part of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 1 – DEFINITIONS AND CONTRACT DOCUMENTS

1.1 “Services” means all analysis, design, development, coding, testing, integration, documentation, deployment assistance, training materials, defect correction and other services expressly described in the SOW.

1.2 “Deliverables” means all software, source code, object code, databases, schemas, APIs, interfaces, designs, models, documentation, manuals, credentials, configurations, test materials, reports, updates, enhancements and other outputs produced under this Agreement.

1.3 In the event of inconsistency, the order of precedence shall be: (a) this Agreement; (b) signed Change Requests; (c) the SOW; (d) approved Financial Plan; and (e) any other annex. No email, meeting note or proposal shall amend this Agreement unless expressly signed as an amendment by both Parties.

## ARTICLE 2 – SUBJECT MATTER, TERM AND INFORMATION ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

2.1 The Second Party shall design, develop and deliver an integrated, commercially operable software system for the First Party in accordance with the SOW and the First Party’s approved requirements.

2.2 The delivery term shall be six (6) months from the Effective Date, unless extended solely by a written Change Request signed by the First Party.

2.3 The Second Party expressly acknowledges and warrants that, before signing this Agreement, it has received and reviewed all information, data, documents, business requirements and technical materials made available by the First Party; has conducted the meetings and discovery activities it considered necessary; has fully understood the nature, objectives and requirements of the Project; and has received information sufficient to determine the scope, implementation period and Contract price.

2.4 The Second Party further acknowledges that, as of the Effective Date, it is not aware of any missing information, technical obstacle, site condition, physical inspection requirement or other impediment that prevents performance of the agreed scope.

2.5 Clarifying questions arising during performance shall not constitute evidence that the information originally provided was insufficient and shall not entitle the Second Party to additional fees, suspension, termination or extension of time unless the First Party has expressly approved a Change Request under Article 4.

2.6 The First Party shall respond to reasonable written clarification requests within three (3) business days.

## ARTICLE 3 – QUALITY, TESTING, ACCEPTANCE AND WARRANTY

3.1 The Second Party shall perform the Services with the professional skill and care expected from an experienced international software developer and shall deliver a secure, scalable, maintainable and commercially usable system conforming to the SOW.

3.2 Deliverables shall be subject to testing and written acceptance by the First Party. Silence, use for testing, partial use, access to a staging environment, or lapse of time shall not constitute deemed acceptance.

3.3 The First Party shall have fourteen (14) business days after a formal delivery to test and either accept the Deliverable or issue a defect/non-conformity list. The acceptance period shall restart after corrected delivery.

3.4 Blocker, Critical and Major defects must be corrected without additional charge within the remediation times agreed in the SOW or, if not stated, within commercially reasonable periods appropriate to severity. Minor and Trivial defects shall also be corrected at no additional charge within an agreed release cycle and shall not be permanently waived merely because they do not prevent provisional use.

3.5 The Second Party warrants all Deliverables for twelve (12) months from final written acceptance. During the warranty period, defects attributable to the Second Party shall be corrected without additional charge.

## ARTICLE 4 – CHANGE REQUESTS AND CLARIFICATIONS

4.1 A Change Request exists only where the First Party requests a material addition, removal or modification outside the agreed SOW.

4.2 The Second Party shall, within five (5) business days of receiving a Change Request, provide a written impact assessment covering scope, time, price, dependencies and risks.

4.3 No Change Request shall be effective, and no additional fee or schedule extension shall arise, unless approved in writing and signed by the First Party.

4.4 Requests for clarification, technical questions, routine refinements, defect corrections, completion of omitted functions already implicit in the SOW, or measures necessary to achieve the agreed purpose shall not constitute Change Requests.

4.5 The Second Party may not suspend performance merely because a clarification is pending, unless the specific unanswered item makes the affected task objectively impossible and the First Party has failed to respond within the three- business-day period after a clear written request. Any suspension shall be limited strictly to the affected task and shall not affect unrelated work.

## ARTICLE 5 – OBLIGATIONS OF THE FIRST PARTY AND NO-TRAVEL PRINCIPLE

5.1 The First Party shall provide the cooperation expressly required by the SOW, nominate authorized contacts, and pay undisputed amounts that become due in accordance with Article 7.

5.2 All Services shall be performed remotely from the Second Party’s own premises using its own personnel, equipment and infrastructure. No on-site visit, physical inspection, travel, accommodation, transportation or presence at the First Party’s premises is required for the agreed scope.

5.3 The Second Party confirms that the information received before signing was sufficient to scope and price the Project without any site visit.

5.4 The First Party shall not bear, reimburse or become liable for any travel, accommodation, flight, transportation, meal, per diem or similar expense of the Second Party or any third party. No future or final visit shall be required as a condition to perform, complete, test, accept or deliver the Project.

## ARTICLE 6 – OBLIGATIONS OF THE SECOND PARTY

6.1 The Second Party shall perform the Services through qualified personnel and remains fully liable for all personnel and approved subcontractors.

6.2 Subcontracting or assignment of any material part of the Services requires the First Party’s prior written approval. Approval shall not relieve the Second Party of responsibility.

6.3 The Second Party shall provide periodic written progress reports showing work completed, percentage completion, remaining work, risks, defects, dependencies and any variance from the approved schedule or budget.

6.4 The Second Party shall comply with all laws and regulations applicable to its performance and to the deployment and operation of the Deliverables in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

6.5 The Second Party shall promptly disclose any circumstance likely to affect delivery, quality, security or cost and shall propose mitigation measures at its own cost where the circumstance is attributable to it.

6.6 Upon each accepted milestone and upon any expiry or termination, the Second Party shall deliver all work-in-progress, source code, repositories, documentation, credentials, configurations and related materials corresponding to amounts paid by the First Party, without withholding or technical lock-in.

## ARTICLE 8 – INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY, SOURCE CODE

8.1 All bespoke Deliverables created specifically for the Project, including source code, designs, databases, documentation and derivative works, shall be exclusively owned by the First Party upon payment of the corresponding accepted milestone.

8.2 The Second Party irrevocably assigns to the First Party all transferable intellectual property rights in such Deliverables to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law and shall procure equivalent assignments from its employees and approved subcontractors.

8.3 Source code and related repositories shall be delivered progressively with each accepted milestone and fully at final acceptance.

8.4 The Second Party shall not incorporate open-source, third- party or licensed components that impose copyleft, disclosure, recurring licence fees, usage restrictions or other material obligations on the First Party without prior written approval. Approved third-party components shall be fully disclosed with licence terms.

8.5 Pre-existing generic tools demonstrably owned by the Second Party before the Effective Date may remain its property, provided they are separately identified in the SOW and the First Party receives a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free licence sufficient to use, maintain, modify and support the Deliverables without dependency on the Second Party.

## ARTICLE 9 – CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION

9.1 Each Party shall protect the other Party’s confidential information and use it solely for performance of this Agreement.

9.2 The Second Party shall apply access controls, least-privilege principles, secure development practices, encryption where appropriate, logging and incident management appropriate to the nature of the Project.

9.3 Personal data shall be processed only on documented instructions from the First Party and in compliance with applicable Saudi data-protection requirements, including the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law and its implementing regulations to the extent applicable.

9.4 Confidentiality obligations survive expiry or termination. Trade secrets, source code and security information shall remain protected for so long as they retain their confidential nature.

## ARTICLE 10 – SAUDI CYBERSECURITY AND REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

10.1 The Second Party warrants that the architecture, software, development process and Deliverables shall comply with all cybersecurity controls, requirements, frameworks and directives issued by the competent authorities in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia that are applicable to the Project, including applicable requirements of the National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA).

10.2 The Second Party shall comply with applicable Saudi requirements relating to personal data, information security, hosting, cloud services, data residency and cross-border transfers to the extent such requirements apply to the Project. No category of applicable Saudi requirement is excluded from this Agreement.

10.3 If a Saudi regulatory requirement applicable to the agreed scope changes during the implementation period, the Second Party shall promptly notify the First Party and implement changes necessary for compliance. Where the change was publicly issued before the Effective Date or reasonably foreseeable by a professional provider, it shall not constitute a Change Request.

10.4 The Second Party shall promptly notify the First Party of any actual or suspected security incident affecting the Project or First Party data and shall cooperate fully in containment, investigation, remediation and legally required notifications.

## ARTICLE 11 – TERMINATION, SUSPENSION AND SETTLEMENT OF COMPLETED WORK

11.1 The First Party may terminate this Agreement for material breach by the Second Party if the breach is not cured within fifteen (15) business days after written notice, or immediately where the breach is incapable of cure, involves confidentiality/security, intellectual property infringement, fraud, abandonment, or repeated material delay.

11.2 The First Party may also terminate the Agreement for convenience upon ten (10) business days’ written notice.

11.3 The Second Party may terminate only for a material and undisputed payment default that continues for twenty (20) business days after written notice. The Second Party may not terminate because of ordinary clarification requests, disagreements, or matters already included in the agreed scope.

11.4 Upon termination, the Second Party is entitled only to the value of work actually completed, delivered and objectively capable of being used by the First Party up to the effective termination date, less amounts already paid and any direct costs of remedying the Second Party’s breach.

11.5 If the Parties dispute the value of completed work, it shall be determined by an independent technical expert jointly appointed within ten (10) business days. If they do not agree on an expert, either Party may request appointment by the competent court or an agreed professional body. Neither Party may unilaterally determine the disputed value.

11.6 The expert shall value only demonstrably completed and usable work by reference to the approved Financial Plan, accepted milestones, repository evidence and actual Deliverables.

11.7 Upon termination, the Second Party shall immediately deliver all paid-for and partially completed work, source code, repositories, credentials and documentation, and provide reasonable transition assistance.

## ARTICLE 12 – LIABILITY, INDEMNITY AND FORCE MAJEURE

12.1 Each Party is liable for direct losses caused by its breach, negligence or unlawful conduct.

12.2 The Second Party shall indemnify the First Party against third-party claims arising from intellectual property infringement, breach of confidentiality, data/security violations attributable to the Second Party, or unlawful acts of its personnel.

12.3 Any contractual liability cap shall not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct, gross negligence, confidentiality breach, data/security breach, or intellectual property infringement. For other claims, the Second Party’s aggregate liability shall not be less than the total Contract price.

12.4 Neither Party shall be liable for failure caused solely by a genuine force majeure event beyond reasonable control, provided the affected Party promptly notifies the other, mitigates the effects and resumes performance as soon as possible. Lack of personnel, subcontractor failure, cost increases, ordinary technical difficulties or foreseeable supply issues shall not constitute force majeure.

## ARTICLE 13 – NON-SOLICITATION, MEETINGS AND NOTICES

13.1 During the term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, neither Party shall knowingly solicit for employment personnel of the other Party who were directly involved in the Project.

13.2 Project meetings shall be conducted remotely unless the First Party determines otherwise. Meeting minutes become binding only when confirmed in writing by authorized representatives of both Parties and may not amend this Agreement unless they expressly state that they are a contractual amendment.

13.3 Formal notices shall be in writing and sent by email to the addresses stated in this Agreement, with delivery capable of being evidenced. Routine operational communications may also be conducted through agreed electronic channels.

## ARTICLE 14 – GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

14.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws and regulations of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

14.2 The Parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, its interpretation, performance, breach, termination, or validity through amicable negotiations within thirty (15 ) days from the date one Party gives the other written notice of the dispute.

14.3 If the dispute is not resolved amicably within the said period, it shall be finally resolved by arbitration seated in the City of Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, in accordance with the Saudi Arbitration Law and its Implementing Regulations.

14.4 The arbitral tribunal shall be constituted in accordance with the Saudi Arbitration Law or as otherwise agreed by the Parties in writing. The arbitral award shall be final and binding upon both Parties and may be enforced before the competent court in accordance with applicable Saudi law.

14.5 Nothing in this Article prevents either Party from applying to the competent court for urgent or interim protective measures where legally permissible, without such application being deemed a waiver of the arbitration agreement.

## ARTICLE 15 – FINAL PROVISIONS, LANGUAGE, AMENDMENTS AND SIGNATURES

15.1 This Agreement, the SOW, approved Financial Plan and signed Change Requests constitute the entire agreement between the Parties concerning the Project and supersede prior proposals or understandings to the extent of inconsistency.

15.2 No amendment, waiver or addition is effective unless made in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

15.3 This Agreement is executed in Arabic and English. Both texts are intended to express the same agreement; however, in the event of inconsistency or ambiguity, the Arabic text shall prevail for purposes of interpretation and enforcement in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

15.4 If any provision is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in force and the invalid provision shall be replaced by a valid provision closest to the original commercial purpose.

15.5 Electronic signatures and counterparts are valid to the extent permitted by applicable Saudi law.
