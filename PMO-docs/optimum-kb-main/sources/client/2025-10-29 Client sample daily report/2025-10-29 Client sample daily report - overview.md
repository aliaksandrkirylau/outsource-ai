## What it is

An Excel template of a "Daily Drilling Fluids Report": header (job, operator, contractor, rig, well),
drilling assembly, wellbore geometry, volumes, pump information, mud properties by sample, solids
analysis, rheology readings at 600 to 3 RPM, hydraulic analysis (n, k, ECD), bit information, solids
control equipment, volume accounting, cost totals and the products used, plus a daily volume report
by pit, a products price list and an empty hydraulics report sheet. Three products carry sample
quantities; nearly everything else is blank.

## How far to trust it

High for what the client's daily report holds today and what it calls things. The client sent it
"for visual appreciation" and expects the product to be "better designed, intuitive and detailed"
(Q&A F29): it is a reference, not the required layout (`sources/client/AGENTS.md`).

## Not for

The required report format, the list of measured parameters (under 50, not confirmed against this
sheet), or cost logic.

## Removed at ingest

Nothing. The cost and price cells are empty; the "USD" labels carry no figures.

## Summary

The client's end-of-day report today is an Excel form with about 30 mud properties, hydraulics and
volume sections, and a product consumption table. The "Hydraulics Report" sheet is empty.
