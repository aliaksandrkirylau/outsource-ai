## What it is

An "End Of Well Recap" for a "Development Well", drilled in 29 days to 18,203 ft MD / 10,285 ft TVD,
horizontal at the end (89.9°). Pages, in order:

- p1 cover; p2 "Recap - Executive Summary": comments per casing interval (losses, pills, cement jobs)
  and a table per interval: days, casing and depth, hole size, maximum mud weight (pcf), angle and
  temperature, frac gradient, fluid type, cost.
- p3 "Well Cost Summary" per interval; p4 "Total Product And Engineering Consumption For The Well", 47
  products by section, with total units and cost.
- Then one set of pages per section - 22" spud mud, 16" NaCl polymer, 12" inhibitive PHPA, 8⅜" NaCl
  polymer, 8⅜" OBM 80/20, and the completion with CaCl2 brine:
  - "Section Fluids": the section header (depths, TVD, deviation, footage, hole volume, drilling hours,
    ROP, flow rates, BHCT/BHST, casing), mud properties at three depths against "Mud Spec's", volume
    accounting, time distribution, mud pumps, lithology, solids control equipment, problems and
    solutions, rig operations;
  - "Costs": drilling-fluid and special-fluid products (LCM, casing-go pill, spacer and others), "OBM
    Transferred In / Out (LMP/Other Well/Other Section)", engineering service, planned units and planned
    cost, section costs, not planned costs, a cost analysis;
  - "Concentrations": ppb per product, with comments;
  - "Property Tracking" (not for the completion): each property against MD, actual against "Plan Low"
    and "Plan High", some "Mud Property not tracked.";
  - "Time, Volume & Cost Summary": volume in and out by source and by loss, mud cost per bbl and per ft,
    the cost breakdown by chemical category with and without weight material.
- Each section's pages close on a daily report number: "DMR 2", "DMR 8", "DMR 14", "DMR 16", "DMR 26",
  "DMR 28".

One standard is named: "Interval Dilution factor | 0.07 | API 13-C, 5.4.9" (p28). No formulas, no
program or vendor name. Mud weight in pcf, concentrations in ppb, chlorides in mg/l and g/l. Operator,
well, contractor, people and dates are empty; the headers of the property tracking pages are blacked
out in the original.

## How far to trust it

High for what the client's end-of-well report holds today and what it calls things. A sample, not the
required layout (sources/client/AGENTS.md). A real well, anonymised by the client; its values are one
well's, not reference values.

The prices, costs and cost shares are kept as printed: the rendering is flagged `money:
client-domain` (Anton's call, 2026-11-02; AGENTS.md rule 7), so the cost arithmetic - unit price by
units used, section and well totals, cost per ft and per bbl, planned against actual - can be checked
against it. They are the client's confidential commercial data: notes cite the rendering, never copy
the figures, and no export carries them.

## Not for

The required report format; thresholds (the specs and plan limits are this well's).

## Summary

The client's end-of-well report is a stack of per-section sheets - properties against spec, volumes,
time, equipment, products, costs, concentrations, charts against plan - under a well summary of
intervals, costs and total consumption. Costs run through every page, planned against actual.
