**22” x 18 ⅝” casing**
- Section was drilled to 689 ft and 18 ⅝’’ casing was cemented.
- Observed bit and BHA in good shape while using Spud Mud without controlling fluid loss.

**16” x 13 ⅜” casing**
- 16’’ section was drilled to section TD @5,055 ft and 13 ⅜’’ casing was ran. Performed cement job succeessfully .
- Recorded partial losses at 1,078 ft. Drilled with 65 pcf LFL spud mud to 2,100 feet then switch to 70 pcf Salt inhibited polymer mud with 60-80% return.
- 250 bbl 95 pcf Casing-Go Pill without Lubricant was spotted at bottom prior POOH.
- Back ream first 5 stands and pump out all the way to 2500 ft due tight spots whilst pumping CGP. Pull on elevators to surface. No indication of bit balling on BHA.
- 5 cement top jobs were performed until cement was observed at surface.

**12” OH x 9 ⅝” casing**
- 12’’ section was drilled to section TD @ 7,330 ft with 70 pcf PHPA inhibited polymer mud. 9 ⅝’’ casing was set on bottom and cemented without any problem.
- 350 bbl of 78 pcf Casing Go Pill with 3.0% Lubricant was spotted on bottom prior POOH.
- Carried ouy cement job in two stages successfully .

**8 3/8” OH (ith WBM) x 7” liner**
- 8 3/8 ’’ section was drilled to section TD @ 9,271 ft with 80 pcf Salt Polymer mud and full return.
- At section TD , Sweep the hole with 2x50 bbl Hi-Vis tprior to POOH. POOH was smooth
- Ran 7"Liner , set at bottom and cemented without any problem.

**8 3/8” OH (with OBM x 5.5” Casing**
- Drilled 8 ⅜" curve section from 9,271 ft to well TD @ 18,203 ft.
- Mud density was gradually increased from 80pcf to 89pcf @ landing point and maintained till TD before further increase to 92pcf.

- Back-reamed and pumped out of hole due to tight spots while POOH.
- Run 5 ½" Casing smoothly to depth @ 18,131 ft; cemented casing successfully.

### Drilling Fluid Description, Maintenance and Comments

Recovered Spud Mud from previous 22’’ section, diluted with water to reduce MBT and treated with Starch to control API FL to have 65 pcf Low Fluid Loss Spud Mud as per program. Drilled cement with water, dumping all returns. Well was displaced with 65 pcf Low Fluid Loss Spud Mud.

16" hole was drilled to 2050 ft (UER formation) by using LFL Spud Mud, pumped 30 bbl of Hi-Vis sweeps every 45 ft drilled, recorded 10-15 % extra cuttings on shakers. Active system was treated with STARCH to reduce fluid loss less than 5 ml/30 minutes.

Encountered partial downhole losses at 1,080 ft; kept mixing LFL Spud mud continuously and swaping active tanks. Drilled 16’’ hole to 2050 ft with partial returns. Active system was converted to 67 pcf Salt Polymer Mud and 16’’ hole was drilled to section TD @ 5055 ft with partial returns, pumping 70 pcf Mud Cap from Trip Tank every connection in the annulus to keep wellbore stable and to prevent any backflow. 30 bbl Hi-Vis sweeps were pumped at every 45 ft ft of formation drilled.

The active system was maintained by regular swaping of tanks whilst mixing fresh mud in Frac tanks. Salt Polymer mud was mixed with 2-4 ppb STARCH, 0.5 ppb Caustic Soda, 30-60 ppb Salt, 0.25 ppb Soda Ash, 0.5-1 ppb PHPA. The MW of Salt Polymer mud in active system was increased at 4200 ft from 67 pcf to 71 pcf progressevely. PHPA was increased concentration from 1 ppb to 1.5 ppb while drilling last 800 ft of 16" hole .

1000 bbl 95 pcf CSG-Go Pill was prepared by addition of 2% Polyamine ( KLA-STOP), 1.5 ppb NEWDRILL along with Salt up to 150 K mg/l, Starch and Barite which had API FL @ 2.6 ml/30 minutes, PV @ 35 cP, YP @ 27 lb/100 ft2 and Salinity @ 160,000 mg/l. Spotted Casing Go Pill on bottom based on Foreman’s request. POOH 16’’ assembly on elevators to surface

Solid Control - Three shakers were used dressed with 80 mesh screens while receiving full mud return. De-sander and De-silter were used continuousely.
