# sources/client/

- What: material from the client: formulas, report samples, screenshots, their documents and answers.
- Trust: high for how the client works today and what they call things. A requirement only once confirmed. Formulas are the client's; check units and ask their subject expert when unclear.
- Not for: treating a sample report as the required report format without confirmation.
- Originals: a PDF of up to 5 MB may sit next to its rendering (procedures/ingest.md step 2), for the charts, scans and layout a rendering cannot carry. Internal reference only.
- Money: a rendering flagged `money: client-domain` keeps the client's prices and costs as printed (AGENTS.md rule 7). They are the client's confidential commercial data: notes cite the rendering, never copy the figures, and no export carries them.
- Autoload: yes.
