**OES | Optimum engineering standards**

Optimum Engineering Standards Co. Ltd., a **Saudi company** specializing in mud engineering and
chemical supply. "OES" is one of the growing business houses established in 2006 and located at
**Al-Khobar** in the **Kingdom of Saudi Arabia**, fully **owned** by the **Saudi Entity**.

**Year founded:** 2006

**Location(s):** Saudi Arabia

**Business Domain(s):**

- Mud Engineering Services
- **Drilling Fluid** & Production Chemical Supply
- **Supply of Oil** & Gas equipment (acting as an agent for pumps, geophones, etc.)

**Industries:** OES is a key subcontractor and service provider in the **Oil** and **Gas**
(**Upstream**) industry.

**Official Website(s):** https://www.oes.com.sa/

**LinkedIn page(s):** -

**Business Model (How client operates & generates revenue):**

- **Specialized Services:** Deploying its team of "Mud Engineers" to rig sites to manage and maintain
  the drilling fluid systems, which are essential for rig safety and efficiency.
- **Chemical & Product Supply:** Supplying a full range of drilling fluids, completion fluids, cement
  additives, and production chemicals.
- **Agency Agreements:** Acting as a local Saudi agent and supplier for international equipment
  manufacturers (e.g., twin-screw pumps, geophones, and Oil Country Tubular Goods).

**Is it B2B / B2C / B2B2C:** B2B. Its clients are large-scale **oil** and **gas extraction** and
production companies, with its primary customer being **Saudi Aramco**.

**Employee count:** N/A

**Ownership:** OES is a privately-held company. The website states it is "fully owned by the Saudi
Entity." Research identifies the President, **CEO**, and Partner as **Dr. Jaber Ayed Al Fahhad**, who
manages a portfolio of companies in the **Saudi energy and industrial sectors**.

**Key Facts**

- **100% Saudi Focus:** OES was established as the "first 100% Saudi Mud Engineers Company," a
  significant strategic position within the Kingdom's "Saudization" and local content initiatives
  (like the IKTVA program).
- **Aramco-Aligned:** The company's QA/QC program and service standards are explicitly benchmarked
  against **Saudi Aramco specifications**, indicating its deep integration into the operations of the
  world's largest oil producer.
- **Specialist, Not Driller:** OES does not own or operate the drilling rigs. It is a crucial
  *subcontractor* that provides the essential "mud engineering" services *on* the rigs (the 70
  locations mentioned). Mud engineering is a highly technical field responsible for the drilling
  fluid, which controls well pressure, cools the drill bit, and removes cuttings.
