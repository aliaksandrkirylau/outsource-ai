## Summary

OES, founded 2006 in Al-Khobar, provides mud engineering services on rigs it does not own, supplies
drilling fluids and chemicals, and acts as an equipment agent. Its main customer is Saudi Aramco and
its QA/QC is benchmarked on Aramco specifications. The first request asked for near real-time
monitoring of drilling fluid properties, data collection for analysis, simulation for planning and
risk mitigation, an intuitive interface, a robust database and mobile app integration.
