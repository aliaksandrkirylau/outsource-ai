## What it is

The BA's "phased vision" (Andrey Lapikov's words in the presale chat, 2025-11-03): 36 feature lines
in five modules, with descriptions and "Optional" or "Architecture Solution" marks, an unfilled workshop
plan, and a list of discovery artifacts. No hours. Kira Barshchevich on 2025-11-03: the team review
that was supposed to follow "так и не состоялось" (never happened).

## How far to trust it

Medium (`sources/presale/`), and superseded by the v1 WBS for scope (batch 4). Useful for the
wording of features and for what was dropped: the sync-monitoring screen, location management,
templates, historical import, and every [+Mobile] item.

## Not for

Scope checks (use the v1 WBS) or the discovery deliverables (the SOW v10.4 lists them differently).

## Removed at ingest

Nothing.

## Summary

Five modules: administration, the engineer's desktop app, supervisor/manager, inventory, reporting.
Mobile variants marked on seven lines. Three roles (Admin, Engineer, Supervisor/Manager). Discovery
planned as workshops ending in a validation meeting, producing a BRD, roadmap, use cases, user stories
with acceptance criteria, data flow and architecture diagrams, wireframes, and optionally test cases
and security documentation.
