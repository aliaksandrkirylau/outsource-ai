**Communication Plan**

| Project stage | Meeting type | Participants | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Kick-off meeting | Customer side: Client, Product Owner, Other involved stakeholders. Contractor side: Project Manager, Business Analyst, UI/UX Designer, Solution Architect | Once | Align on objectives, scope, roles, timeline, and success criteria |
| | Requirements clarification workshops | Customer side: Client (Optional), Product Owner, Other involved stakeholders. Contractor side: Business Analyst, Project Manager, Solution Architect | Twice a week | Capture expectations, constraints, requirements, risks, and success definitions |
| | Design sessions | Customer side: Product Owner, Other involved stakeholders. Contractor side: UI/UX Designer, Business Analyst, Project Manager | Once a week | Review UI Kit, mockups, gather feedback, and ensure alignment |
| | Status report | Customer side: Client, Product Owner, Other involved stakeholders. Contractor side: Project Manager, UI/UX Designer, Business Analyst, Solution Architect | Once a week | Share progress, blockers, communicate findings and risks |
| | Team Sync / Stand-up | Contractor side: Project Manager, Business Analyst, UI/UX Designer, Solution Architect | Twice a week | Share progress, blockers, and next steps |
| | Discovery Readout / Playback | Customer side: Client, Product Owner, Other involved stakeholders. Contractor side: Project Manager, Business Analyst, UI/UX Designer, Solution Architect | The end of the discovery stage | Present deliverables, confirm alignment, and seek approval to proceed |
| Development | Kick-off | Customer side: Client, Product Owner, Other involved stakeholders. Contractor side: Project Manager, Business Analyst, UI/UX Designer, Dev team, QA engineer | Once | Align on project vision, scope, roles, responsibilities, timelines, communication model, and success criteria |
| | Requirements clarification workshops | Customer side: Client (Optional), Product Owner, Other involved stakeholders. Contractor side: Business Analyst, Project Manager | Twice a week | Collect and clarify requirements, approve already specified requirements |
| | Sprint planning | Customer side: Product Owner (Optional). Contractor side: Project Manager, Business Analyst, Dev Team, QA Engineer | Once per Sprint | Define sprint goal, prioritize backlog items, estimate effort, and agree on sprint scope |
| | Daily meeting | Contractor side: Project Manager, Business Analyst, Dev Team, QA Engineer | Daily | Synchronize team activities, identify blockers, and plan work for the next 24 hours |
| | Sprint review | Customer side: Client, Product Owner, Other Involved Stakeholders. Contractor side: Project Manager, Business Analyst, UI/UX Designer, Dev Team, QA Engineer | End of Sprint | Demonstrate completed work, gather feedback, and validate deliverables |
| | Sprint retro | Customer side: Product Owner (Optional). Contractor side: Project Manager, Business Analyst, Dev Team, QA Engineer | End of Sprint | Inspect team performance, identify improvements, and agree on action items |
| | Backlog Refinement | Customer side: Product Owner (Optional). Contractor side: Project Manager, Business Analyst, Dev Team, QA Engineer | 1- 2 times per Sprint | Clarify requirements, refine user stories, estimate backlog items, and prepare for next sprint |
| | Status report meeting with Client | Customer side: Client, Product Owner, Other Involved Stakeholders. Contractor side: Project Manager, Delivery Manager (optional) | Monthly/ On demand | Review overall project status, risks, milestones, and decision-making. Discuss delivery progress, resourcing, and escalation management |
