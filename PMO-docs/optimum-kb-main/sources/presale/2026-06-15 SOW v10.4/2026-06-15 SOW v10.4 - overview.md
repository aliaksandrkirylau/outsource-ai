# 2026-06-15 SOW v10.4 - overview

kind: presale, by Anton's call on 2026-09-28: the SOW is not confirmed as the signed contract's Annex 1,
so it is stored here, not in `sources/contract/` (see that folder's AGENTS.md). Rendering:
[[2026-06-15 SOW v10.4 - rendering]].

## What it is

The fixed-price Statement of Work: parties, description, desktop and web modules, two phases split into
six one-month milestones with detailed deliverables per milestone, assumptions and constraints, dates,
the fee schedule, payment and termination terms, the Contractor's responsibility. Bilingual English and
Arabic.

## How far to trust it

Medium, and the best scope text there is. The contract (Article 2.1, 15.6) says the system is delivered
"in accordance with the SOW" and lists "Annex 1 – Statement of Work (SOW)", but no annex is attached to
the contract PDF, and several things say this SOW was not written for the final contract:

- It is "issued pursuant to ... the Software Development Services Contract No. (1/26) dated 15/05/2026";
  the final contract carries no number or date.
- It gives the client's Commercial Registration No. as (2051032302); the final contract as (7001489900).
- It sends acceptance to "clauses 7.9 – 7.10 of the Agreement", which in the final contract are the
  monthly progress report and "No additional cost above the maximum Contract price"; acceptance is 3.2
  to 3.3 there.
- It pays the advance "in accordance with Clause 4.6 of the Agreement"; the final contract has no 4.6
  (Article 4 ends at 4.5; the advance is 7.3). A clause 4.6 on the advance-payment timeline is in the
  Framework Agreement of December 2025 ([[2025-12-30 Answers to the client's questions - rendering]]).
- Its fee schedule differs from the contract's Article 7, and its termination and extension terms
  differ too (batch 3 rows 1, 12 and 16).

Where it disagrees with the contract, the notes keep both; the contract's own order of precedence (1.3)
puts the Agreement above the SOW. Which SOW is Annex 1 is the proposed Question QUE-006 (batch 3
row 20).

## Not for

Amounts (removed; they are in the original and the P&L), or treating a clause as binding without the
contract.

## Removed at ingest

Every money amount (the total, the milestone costs, the advance), the Arabic text, phone numbers and
e-mail addresses in the parties block, the empty signature lines.

## Summary

Desktop (Windows) and web only; mobile, responsive layout, API and external integrations, the in-app
wizard and user manuals, data migration all out; English only; four roles assumed, three predefined in
Milestone 2. Milestone 1 is discovery (vision and scope document, solution architecture, UI kit and
mockups, communication plan, revised WBS, project plan); Milestones 2 to 6 build auth and users, user
and project management with sync, inventory and thresholds, calculations and the web dashboard, the
desktop dashboard, reporting and audit logs, then final handover. The customer provides
infrastructure, third-party services, data, formulas and the compliance requirements. About six months
from a start date agreed by e-mail.
