kind: presale. original: Google Slides "Proposal from Innowise - Optimum Engineering Standards", 40
slides, created 2025-11-05, last changed 2026-05-15. Rendering: [[2025-11-05 Proposal slides - rendering]].

## What it is

The client-facing proposal: overview, objectives, functional requirements, architecture, team,
delivery approach, then three iterations of timeline and budget, each with its "key optimization
areas", and post go-live support. When each iteration was shown to the client is not in the deck; the
presale chat (batch 5) has it.

The iterations, in short:

1. Initial: the full scope, on-premises objective, but slide 14 already says "centralized cloud
   control" with "AWS PostgreSQL Database ... Deployed at OES HQ".
2. Mobile out of scope, the desktop limited to input only with everything else in the web app, cloud
   instead of hybrid or on-premises, calculations and simulations out of scope.
3. Calculations and simulations back ("samples were provided"), a unit management system added, a
   discount, and special pricing conditions: start in the first week of January 2026, success-story
   rights after go-live, an advance before start.
