# 2025-12-30 Answers to the client's questions - overview

kind: presale. original: Google Doc "Client questions", created 2025-12-29, changed 2025-12-30. It is
the text of the reply; whether and when it was e-mailed is not recorded in the doc. Rendering:
[[2025-12-30 Answers to the client's questions - rendering]].

## What it is

Innowise's answers to the client's round of questions on the SOW and the Framework Agreement of the
time: costs, warranty, AWS credits, communication, calculations and offline limits, API, responsive
design, support, payment terms. The client's own questions survive as the text the internal comments
quote.

## How far to trust it

Medium, and high for what we told the client in December 2025. The final contract (batch 3) supersedes
every term here; the technical statements (calculations in the app, offline limits) still describe the
design.

## Not for

Current commercial or legal terms.

## Removed at ingest

The late-payment interest rate (twice), a currency figure in the AWS-credits comment, and a share of
the project cost in the Framework Agreement comment.

## Summary

No costs beyond the SOW and the Framework Agreement without a formal change; warranty raised to six
weeks; e-mail as the main channel and requirements approved in writing; calculations hard-coded in the
desktop app from the client's formulas, with the offline limits that follow; web reports show the last
synced data; API and responsive design only by separate estimate; the web app designed for a
1440x1024 desktop frame; payment term 14 working days, advance within 10 calendar days, governing law
KSA, annual renewal removed. The client had asked for three milestone installments every two months.
