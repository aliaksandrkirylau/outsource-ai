# sources/

- What: everything that came in, stored once and never edited. One subfolder per kind; each has its own AGENTS.md.
- Trust: a source is what someone said or wrote, not what was agreed. Weight it by its subfolder's note.
- Not for: editing. Originals, renderings and transcripts stay as stored; a correction is a new file saying what it corrects. Overviews and minutes are ours: a row's result, added links and pointers may be updated. Our own placeholders may be swapped for the link in any file. These AGENTS.md trust notes are schema, changed by the curator and logged. Money amounts, personal data beyond names and roles, and credentials are removed at ingest.
- Autoload: yes.
