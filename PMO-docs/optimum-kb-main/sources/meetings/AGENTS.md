# sources/meetings/

- What: one folder per meeting, internal or with the client: the verbatim transcript (Bluedot, or notes when unrecorded) and our minutes.
- Trust: the transcript is high for who said what. The minutes are our reading; check the transcript for exact wording. An agreement counts only if it was made explicitly.
- Not for: treating a demo, a nod or "sounds good" as acceptance.
- Autoload: yes.
