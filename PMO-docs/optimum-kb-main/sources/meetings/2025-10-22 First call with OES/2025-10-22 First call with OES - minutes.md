## Summary

The client (about 70 rig locations, Saudi Aramco its biggest customer) outgrew Excel and ready-made
tools and wants its own software. Engineers measure drilling-fluid properties about 4 times a day and
write reports. The client asked for a Windows desktop app used jointly on a rig computer, with a
mobile app later for supervisors, and asked Innowise for advice on desktop or web and on storage
(central database, not only cloud). Features listed: users and permissions, projects, measurements,
inventory, threshold alerts, dashboards, reports with PDF, CSV and XLS export, calculations and
"experimenting", hydraulics simulation. Out of scope: sensors, integrations, API design,
predictive-maintenance simulation.
