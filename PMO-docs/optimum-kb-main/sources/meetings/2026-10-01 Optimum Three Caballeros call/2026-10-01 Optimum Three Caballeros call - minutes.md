## Summary

Alexey took the PM picture as clear - the problem, the rough dates, the resource plan - and walked the
team through the technical scheme: a Flutter desktop thick client on Windows at the rig, an online
service at HQ, eventual consistency between them. Open points he named: how desktop builds reach the
rigs, whether calculations run anywhere but the desktop, whether past results are recalculated when a
formula changes, how two-way sync works, and where the web service is hosted. Arseniy's team was still
drawing the architecture; sync was described as an outbox on the desktop and pull with
acknowledgement from HQ, "типовые задачи". On hosting, AWS is out and Alexey proposed pitching Google
Cloud, since the client is unlikely to provide a data centre certified for the Saudi cybersecurity
rules. Mikhail raised two contract risks: a formula bug that damages a well is our liability, and the
client may try to put the cloud costs on us. Nothing was decided. Next call next week, same format.

After the call, at 12:56 UTC, Arseniy posted his team's diagrams of the self-hosted Supabase variant.
Mikhail's diagram in the call's chat was generated from this KB and is "не финальный source of truth".
