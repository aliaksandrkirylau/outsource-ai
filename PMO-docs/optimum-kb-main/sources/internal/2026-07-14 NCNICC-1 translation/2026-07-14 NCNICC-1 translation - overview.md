An English translation of the National Cybersecurity Authority's controls for private-sector entities
not considered critical infrastructure (NCNICC-1:2025): "перевод с официального документа, кот. доступен
тольна на арабском (перевод делалася Claude). оставляю здесь для переиспользования" (Alexey Gamanovich,
[[2025-11-25 Contract chat - rendering 3]] 2026-07-14 12:00).

## How far to trust it

Low as a legal text: a machine translation of an Arabic original, not checked by a translator. Good
enough to find which controls to read; the Arabic original binds.

## Not for

Legal interpretation or a compliance claim; see [[CON-007 Saudi cybersecurity and data protection rules]]
and [[COM-015 Saudi cybersecurity and regulatory compliance]].
