# REQ-011 Dashboards and KPIs

## Need

Managers see the state of all active rigs at a glance, and engineers see their own rig's key figures.
The client's KPIs ([[2025-10-29 Client answers to the Q&A register - rendering]] F.27):

1. "Tracking of critical mud parameter by depth & compared to offset wells."
2. "Top 5 product consumption and general products use pie chart for current well section &
   cumulative for the well."
3. "Fluids volume trend, cumulative mud loss and volume-out alert."
4. "QHSE alerts: alerts on upcoming expirations of products, reagents, stock- outs, or equipment
   calibration."
5. "Drilling progress status"

Timeframe: "Per 12-hour shift every operating day on all active locations (rigs)." (F.28)

Scope: contracted. v1 WBS ([[2026-09-25 v1 WBS - rendering]]) Development Flutter rows 31 (web Dashboard View) and 56 (desktop Dashboard
View), both estimated.

## Acceptance criteria

To be written by the BA.

## Edge cases

| Scenario | Expected behaviour |
|---|---|
| KPI 1 compares with offset wells, but offset-well data was never entered | Not defined. Historical import is out of scope ([[REQ-015 Import of historical data]]) and data is kept 1-2 years (C.8), so offset-well data may not exist in the system |
| A rig has not synced for days | The web shows the last synced data: "any reports generated by the user on the web app side will be based on the last synchronized data. The data will only be updated once the connection is restored" ([[2025-12-30 Answers to the client's questions - rendering]], Page 2). The first request's "~real-time monitoring" ([[2025-10-23 OES company research - rendering]]) was withdrawn by the client on the first call: "So I'll just say forget about the term real time. Okay. I guess what Zara was referring to is that whatever data that has been manually imputed into the system, that data is finally saved. It should be available to be seen by authorized users" ([[2025-10-22 First call with OES - transcript]] 0:33:11); "a supervisor in the office should be able to see it real time" once the engineer has finished a report (0:34:40) |

## Business rules

- The web view: "a high-level one-page snapshot of operational status, key KPIs, and progress against
  targets across multiple job sites. Includes filter and search for a particular project"
  ([[2025-10-28 BA draft WBS - rendering]] row 26).
- Charts are estimated as simple types (v1 WBS, Assumptions & Risks 11); see
  [[QUE-003 Do calculations and modelling need 3D or complex visuals]].
- "Manager: Access dashboards, oversee multiple sites" (D.13).
- On the first call the client found it "too early to define the nature of the dashboard", but
  sketched it: "a one page summary of what is going on with that operation at, at that point in time
  ... It should be able to tell this is the current status against the target and this is what needs
  to still happen" ([[2025-10-22 First call with OES - transcript]] 0:39:42, 0:40:30).
- The client's end-of-well report already charts, per section (added 2026-11-02;
  [[2026-11-02 Client sample end-of-well recap - rendering]] pp [[2026-11-02 Client sample end-of-well recap - EOWR Sample.pdf#page=8|8]], [[2026-11-02 Client sample end-of-well recap - EOWR Sample.pdf#page=12|12]], [[2026-11-02 Client sample end-of-well recap - EOWR Sample.pdf#page=13|13]], [[2026-11-02 Client sample end-of-well recap - EOWR Sample.pdf#page=17|17]], [[2026-11-02 Client sample end-of-well recap - EOWR Sample.pdf#page=18|18]]): each property
  against depth with plan limits, volume in and out by source and by loss, mud cost per bbl and per
  ft, and the cost breakdown by chemical category with and without weight material. Evaluated: close
  to KPI 1 (properties by depth, against plan rather than offset wells) and KPI 2 (a pie by chemical
  category, of cost rather than consumption); report charts, not a dashboard.
