# TRM-008 Mud check

## Definition

One complete set of drilling-fluid measurements taken from a sample by the [[TRM-003 Mud engineer]]
with lab equipment - rheology, chemical titrations and the rest - and entered into the software. In
the client's words: "each person will be required to put in two complete mud check, okay, every six
hours, you know, so at the end of the day you should have four complete mud checks ... those four mod
checks are what is going to constitute, you know, a single daily mock report for that day. It's not
going to be. Each mod check is not a report in and of itself"
([[2025-10-22 First call with OES - transcript]] 0:42:22).

## Context and units

- Frequency: "the industry standards is usually three to four times" a day; two per engineer per
  12-hour [[TRM-002 Shift]], one every six hours (0:42:22).
- How it is measured: "We have lab equipment, okay, that are set up and utilized by the engineer ... it
  is not sensor based"; for viscosity the engineer "gets a sample of the fluid and he runs through the
  entire process of determining ... the complete rheology profile"; some checks "involve chemical
  analysis ... chemical titrations" (0:22:25).
- Size: "less than 50" parameters (0:41:51).
- Speech recognition writes it "mod check" and "mock report".
- The property set depends on the mud (added 2026-11-02;
  [[2026-11-02 Client sample end-of-well recap - rendering]]): the water-based sheets have MBT, Pm,
  Pf, Mf and API filtrate; the oil-based section adds HTHP filtrate at 300 °F, electrical stability,
  O/W ratio and the CaCl2 water phase ([[2026-11-02 Client sample end-of-well recap - EOWR Sample.pdf#page=24|p24]]); the completion brine adds TSS and turbidity ([[2026-11-02 Client sample end-of-well recap - EOWR Sample.pdf#page=29|p29]]). The
  daily report [[2026-11-02 Client sample daily mud reports - DMR Sample 1.pdf|DMR Sample 1]] lists 36 properties
  ([[2026-11-02 Client sample daily mud reports - rendering]]), within "less than 50".
- A sample records where and when it was taken: "Sample From" Active Pit, Flow Line and "Check Time"
  ([[2026-11-02 Client sample daily mud reports - DMR Sample 1.pdf|DMR Sample 1]]); "Time sample taken" ([[2026-11-02 Client sample daily mud reports - DMR Sample 2.pdf|DMR Sample 2]]).

## Not to be confused with

- A report ([[TRM-001 Report]]): four mud checks make one daily report.
- A sensor reading: there are no sensors (0:22:25).
