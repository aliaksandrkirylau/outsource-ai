# TRM-004 Rig

## Definition

A drilling rig where the client's [[TRM-003 Mud engineer]] works; one "location" in the client's
count of about 70 ([[2025-10-29 Client answers to the Q&A register - rendering]] Part 1, 7). Each rig
has its own computer: "The computer is assigned to that particular drilling rig. The computer is not
assigned to the individuals" ([[2025-10-22 First call with OES - notes]] key point 4).

## Context and units

- About 70 today, "can potentially reach 100" (Part 1, 7).
- OES does not own the rigs: it "provides the essential 'mud engineering' services on the rigs"
  ([[2025-10-23 OES company research - rendering]]). Its customers are oil and gas companies, Saudi
  Aramco the largest; who owns or operates each rig is not stated.
- A rig drills one well after another; the same computer stays with the rig across wells (notes, key
  point 4).
- The sources use "location", "job site", "rig site", "site" and "work location" for the same thing.

## Not to be confused with

- A well or a [[TRM-005 Project (well job)]]: a rig drills many wells.
- The client's HQ, where the central database sits (Part 1, 8).
