# TRM-006 Well section

## Definition

A part of a well that the client reports on separately: End-of-Section reports need sign-off, and
consumption is tracked "per section and well"
([[2025-10-29 Client answers to the Q&A register - rendering]] Part 1, 3; D.14; E.24).

[ASSUMPTION: a section is a hole section, the stretch of the well drilled with one bit size between
two casing points. No source defines it; this is the usual drilling meaning, to confirm with the
client's subject expert.]

## Context and units

- End-of-Section and End-of-Well reports are the only ones that need approval (D.14).
- KPI: "Top 5 product consumption and general products use pie chart for current well section &
  cumulative for the well" (F.27).
- The client's daily report has a "SEC." field in the drilling assembly block
  ([[2025-10-29 Client sample daily report - rendering]] row 9); what it holds is not stated.
- The client's end-of-well report is split by hole section (added 2026-11-02;
  [[2026-11-02 Client sample end-of-well recap - rendering]] pp [[2026-11-02 Client sample end-of-well recap - EOWR Sample.pdf#page=2|2]], [[2026-11-02 Client sample end-of-well recap - EOWR Sample.pdf#page=3|3]]), each named by hole and casing
  size ("16” x 13 ⅜” casing", "8 3/8” OH (ith WBM) x 7” liner"), with its own mud system, spec and
  costs, plus a "Completion" section in CaCl2 brine with no new hole ([[2026-11-02 Client sample end-of-well recap - EOWR Sample.pdf#page=29|p29]]). It supports the
  assumption above and adds the completion as a section of its own. The daily reports have "Section
  Name/No" ([[2026-11-02 Client sample daily mud reports - DMR Sample 1.pdf|DMR Sample 1]]) and "Section" ([[2026-11-02 Client sample daily mud reports - DMR Sample 2.pdf|DMR Sample 2]])
  ([[2026-11-02 Client sample daily mud reports - rendering]]).

## Not to be confused with
