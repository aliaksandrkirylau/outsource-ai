# TRM-003 Mud engineer

## Definition

The client's engineer on a [[TRM-004 Rig]], who looks after the drilling fluid: measures its
properties, enters them, runs calculations and simulations, and produces reports. The client's own
words for the role: "Mud Engineer: Enter measurements, perform calculations and simulations, generate
reports and email reports to the supervisors"
([[2025-10-29 Client answers to the Q&A register - rendering]] D.13). OES deploys "its team of 'Mud
Engineers' to rig sites to manage and maintain the drilling fluid systems"
([[2025-10-23 OES company research - rendering]]).

## Context and units

- In the system the role is "Engineer" ([[2025-10-28 BA draft WBS - rendering]] row 3), the only role
  that uses the desktop app.
- The client also says "field engineer" (C.10); our first-call notes say "sites engineer"
  ([[2025-10-22 First call with OES - notes]] key point 5).
- About two per rig, working 12-hour shifts ([[TRM-002 Shift]]).
- On the first call the client says "the Drilling FLUID ENGINEER" and "job sites engineers"
  ([[2025-10-22 First call with OES - transcript]] 0:47:56, 0:25:39). Engineers rotate: over a year one
  rig computer may see "10 engineers at various point in time, maximum two at the same time" (0:59:46).

## Not to be confused with

- Project Engineers, which the client lists among those who access reports (C.10): a different role,
  not mapped yet ([[REQ-004 Roles and what each role can do]]).
- The engineers on our delivery team.
