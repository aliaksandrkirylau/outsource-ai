# TRM-002 Shift

## Definition

A work shift of an on-site engineer: opened on the desktop, filled with readings as work goes on,
and closed. "Ты в него просто открываешь рабочую смену, работаешь, вносишь показатели ... и
закрываешь" (Arseniy, [[2026-09-25 Optimum Eng sync - transcript]] 00:08:12). Its content becomes a
[[TRM-001 Report]].

## Context and units

Arseniy's description, not the client's. What readings a shift holds is not in this source.

Length (added 2026-09-28): the client monitors "Per 12-hour shift every operating day on all active
locations (rigs)." ([[2025-10-29 Client answers to the Q&A register - rendering]] F.28). Readings are
taken about 4 times a day (Part 1, 2); the client also wants "In-app accounts per user/shift" (D.11).

How the day runs (added 2026-09-28, batch 2): "it's a 24 hour operation every single day until you
complete the job. And usually you may have two personnel working the job in the 12 hour shift"
([[2025-10-22 First call with OES - transcript]] 0:42:22); "They work 12 hour shift between themselves.
And each person will go into the system and put in all the details of this work during his shift"
(1:06:48). Each engineer does two [[TRM-008 Mud check]]s per shift.

## Not to be confused with

The client's End-of-Day report, which covers a day ([[TRM-001 Report]]).
