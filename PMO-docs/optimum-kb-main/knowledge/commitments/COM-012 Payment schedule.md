# COM-012 Payment schedule

## What is owed

Payments by the client for completed and accepted work. No figures here; they are in Drive and the
P&L. Three schedules are on file and disagree. All three are kept for the PMs; none is picked.

### Version A - the contract (Article 7)

- An advance payment, capped both as a share of the price and as an amount (7.3).
- "The remaining payments shall be distributed over the six (6)- month Contract implementation period."
  A payment "shall become due at the end of each of the first five (5) months, provided that the work
  scheduled for the relevant month has been actually completed in accordance with the approved SOW and
  progress plan." (7.4)
- "Each monthly payment is expressly conditional upon verified completion of the work allocated to that
  month. Mere passage of time, staffing allocation, hours incurred, or submission of an invoice shall
  not by itself create any entitlement to payment." (7.5)
- The final payment "due only upon completion of the sixth month, full delivery of all final
  Deliverables, successful completion of final testing, satisfaction of the agreed quality and
  performance requirements, correction of all material defects, and the First Party's final written
  acceptance" (7.6); the schedule in 7.7: advance, end of months 1 to 5, final payment at the end of
  month 6.
- Disputed invoices or percentages may be withheld "without such withholding constituting a payment
  default" (7.12); a report with each invoice (7.9, [[COM-011 Progress reports and early warning]]).
- "The Parties agree that the Financial Plan set out in this Article shall be incorporated into and form
  an integral part of the Statement of Work (SOW)." (7.2)

([[2026-09-07 Final contract - rendering]])

### Version B - the SOW v10.4 fee schedule

A discounted total; an advance payment "in accordance with Clause 4.6 of the Agreement" (a clause the
final contract does not have); a cost per milestone for Milestones 1, 2, 3 and 4. Milestone 5's cost
cell has no cost, only: "Milestones 4 and 5 shall be deemed covered by the Advance Payment previously
paid by the Customer and shall not require separate interim payments. The value of such Milestones
shall be accounted for against the Advance Payment balance." So Milestone 4 has both a cost and a line
saying it needs no payment. Milestone 6 has no cost of its own: its cell repeats the Milestone 5 text,
ending "against the Advance Deposit balance" ([[2026-06-15 SOW v10.4 - rendering]] rows 16, 36).
Corrected 2026-09-28 (a query): batch 3 had costs for Milestones 1 to 3 only, and cited rows 19-24,
which the rendering does not have; the fee table is in row 16.

### Version C - the plan sent to the client on 2026-03-11

An advance payment, then a payment each month from April to September, "discounted"
([[2026-03-11 Financial plan sent to the client - rendering]]).

### History

In December 2025 the client asked to "Replace monthly invoicing with three (3) milestone-based
installments, payable every two months upon achieving agreed milestones" and a payment term of
fourteen working days; Innowise answered "Milestones were listed into the agreement" and "The payment
term was extended to fourteen (14) working days from the invoice date"
([[2025-12-30 Answers to the client's questions - rendering]]).

From the contract chat, without figures (added 2026-09-28, batch 6):

- November 2025: time and materials with a budget cap and an advance payment (PMO's finalisation,
  [[2025-11-25 Contract chat - rendering 1]] 2025-11-28 19:49).
- March 2026: the client wanted a payment every two months, which clashed with time and materials and
  with Poland's KSeF invoicing from April 2026 (internal meeting, 2026-03-04 12:21); "Клиент дал апрув на
  колле на то, чтобы мы вписали (monthly invoicing with one-month payment delay)" (2026-03-05 12:10).
- 22 May 2026: fixed price with milestones ([[DEC-008 Fixed price with milestones, on the client's contract]]).
- 7 July: the client would accept the advance only "with an unconditional bank guarantee covering the
  full amount of the advance payment", otherwise a capped share up front and "all subsequent payments
  being directly linked to verified project milestones and actual completion percentages"
  ([[2025-11-25 Contract chat - rendering 3]] 2026-07-07 13:13); Innowise's counter-offer on 14 July
  (15:18).
- The client's final contract: a smaller advance, then fixed monthly payments "строго при условии 100%
  выполнения запланированного объема (фактически отработанные часы больше не учитываются)" (Lizaveta
  Shauchenka's summary, 2026-08-12 13:28).

Disputed (above). Month numbers count from the start of delivery, which depends on

## How it is accepted

Contract: each payment on verified completion; the final one on final written acceptance
([[COM-002 Written acceptance of each deliverable]]). The contract's precedence (1.3) puts the
Agreement above the SOW and the Financial Plan; Annex 2, "Approved Financial Plan and Milestones", is
not attached ([[QUE-006 Which SOW and which plan are Annex 1 and Annex 2]]).
