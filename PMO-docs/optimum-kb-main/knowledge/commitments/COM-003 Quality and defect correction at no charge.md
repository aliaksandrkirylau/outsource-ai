# COM-003 Quality and defect correction at no charge

## What is owed

"The Second Party shall perform the Services with the professional skill and care expected from an
experienced international software developer and shall deliver a secure, scalable, maintainable and
commercially usable system conforming to the SOW." ([[2026-09-07 Final contract - rendering]] 3.1)

"Blocker, Critical and Major defects must be corrected without additional charge ... Minor and
Trivial defects shall also be corrected at no additional charge within an agreed release cycle and
shall not be permanently waived merely because they do not prevent provisional use." (3.4)

## By when

"Within the remediation times agreed in the SOW or, if not stated, within commercially reasonable
periods appropriate to severity" (3.4). The SOW v10.4 states no remediation times.

## How it is accepted

Through acceptance of the corrected delivery ([[COM-002 Written acceptance of each deliverable]]).
