# COM-014 Confidentiality and personal data

## What is owed

- "Each Party shall protect the other Party's confidential information and use it solely for
  performance of this Agreement." (9.1)
- "The Second Party shall apply access controls, least-privilege principles, secure development
  practices, encryption where appropriate, logging and incident management appropriate to the nature of
  the Project." (9.2)
- "Personal data shall be processed only on documented instructions from the First Party and in
  compliance with applicable Saudi data-protection requirements, including the Saudi Personal Data
  Protection Law and its implementing regulations to the extent applicable." (9.3)
- Confidentiality survives the contract; trade secrets, source code and security information stay
  protected as long as they remain confidential (9.4).
