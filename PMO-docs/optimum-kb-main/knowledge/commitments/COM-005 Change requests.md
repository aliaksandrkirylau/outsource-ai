# COM-005 Change requests

## What is owed

How scope changes, per the contract ([[2026-09-07 Final contract - rendering]]):

- "A Change Request exists only where the First Party requests a material addition, removal or
  modification outside the agreed SOW." (4.1)
- "The Second Party shall, within five (5) business days of receiving a Change Request, provide a
  written impact assessment covering scope, time, price, dependencies and risks." (4.2)
- "No Change Request shall be effective, and no additional fee or schedule extension shall arise,
  unless approved in writing and signed by the First Party." (4.3); likewise no cost above the maximum
  price without such a CR (7.10).
- Not change requests: "Requests for clarification, technical questions, routine refinements, defect
  corrections, completion of omitted functions already implicit in the SOW, or measures necessary to
  achieve the agreed purpose" (4.4).
- No suspension while a clarification is pending, unless the unanswered item makes the task
  "objectively impossible" and the client missed the three-business-day answer (4.5).
- Clarifying questions do not entitle Innowise "to additional fees, suspension, termination or
  extension of time unless the First Party has expressly approved a Change Request" (2.5).

The SOW v10.4 agrees in substance: "Any deviation from the requirements beyond the services listed
here is considered out of scope and will be treated as a 'Change Request.' It must be obtained
separately through a formal written amendment" ([[2026-06-15 SOW v10.4 - rendering]] row 9).

## By when

Impact assessment within five business days.

Signed by the client.
