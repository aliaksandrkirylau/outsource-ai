# COM-006 Information sufficient for scope and price

## What is owed

A warranty Innowise gives by signing ([[2026-09-07 Final contract - rendering]]):

- "before signing this Agreement, it has received and reviewed all information, data, documents,
  business requirements and technical materials made available by the First Party; has conducted the
  meetings and discovery activities it considered necessary; has fully understood the nature,
  objectives and requirements of the Project; and has received information sufficient to determine the
  scope, implementation period and Contract price." (2.3)
- "as of the Effective Date, it is not aware of any missing information, technical obstacle, site
  condition, physical inspection requirement or other impediment that prevents performance of the
  agreed scope." (2.4)
- "the information received before signing was sufficient to scope and price the Project without any
  site visit." (5.3)
- Clarifying questions later "shall not constitute evidence that the information originally provided
  was insufficient" (2.5).

## By when

On signing; 2.4 as of the Effective Date.
