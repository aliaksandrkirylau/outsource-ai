# COM-011 Progress reports and early warning

## What is owed

- "periodic written progress reports showing work completed, percentage completion, remaining work,
  risks, defects, dependencies and any variance from the approved schedule or budget"
  ([[2026-09-07 Final contract - rendering]] 6.3).
- "with each monthly invoice a written progress report evidencing the work completed during that
  month, the corresponding Deliverables, the percentage of completion, outstanding items, defects, and
  any deviation from the approved schedule" (7.9).
- "promptly disclose any circumstance likely to affect delivery, quality, security or cost and shall
  propose mitigation measures at its own cost where the circumstance is attributable to it" (6.5).

## By when

With each monthly invoice; the other reports "periodic", frequency not set.

Meetings, as said on the introductory call of 20 October and not confirmed in writing
([[COM-018 Written minutes, notices and amendments]]; added 2026-10-30,
[[2026-10-20 Introductory call - transcript]]): Discovery "like 4 or 5 weeks", "probably 2 or 3
meetings a week"; later "something like a weekly status meeting, plus probably monthly demos and
milestone meetings to confirm everything" (Anton, 00:28:59). Mr. Lawal: "I'll prefer at least some
level of engagement, you know, ideally once a week, even if it's going to be a short meeting of 30
minutes", and "I don't think it's healthy for us not to have any engagement in more than a 2-week
period" (00:30:10). A short monthly call between Kavalchuk and Mr. Lawal: "That will be fine."
(00:31:19, 00:31:37)
