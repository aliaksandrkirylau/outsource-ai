# COM-001 Delivery within six months

## What is owed

"An integrated, commercially operable software system for the First Party in accordance with the SOW
and the First Party's approved requirements" ([[2026-09-07 Final contract - rendering]] 2.1).

## By when

Disputed: the contract and the SOW v10.4 set the term differently. Both kept for the PMs.

- Contract: "The delivery term shall be six (6) months from the Effective Date, unless extended solely
  by a written Change Request signed by the First Party." (2.2) The Effective Date is not defined:
  [[QUE-007 What is the Effective Date]].
- SOW v10.4: "The start date for the provision of services shall be agreed upon by the Parties via
  email." "The project duration is approximately six (6) months from the start date. The timeline may
  be extended if unexpected difficulties arise during development or if the client or a third party
  causes any delays ... The timeline may also be extended if, upon completion of the Discovery Phase,
  additional work is determined to be necessary" ([[2026-06-15 SOW v10.4 - rendering]] row 14).

The contract's order of precedence puts the Agreement above the SOW (1.3).

Where the term stands since the client signed on 7 October 2026 (added 2026-10-13):
[[QUE-007 What is the Effective Date]]; the risk,
[[K-3 The six months run from the client's signature, before discovery starts]].

When the client's input is late, the SOW v10.4 also gives Innowise a right to suspend the affected work
and to be paid for idle time; the contract does not (added 2026-09-28, a query):
[[COM-008 Client cooperation and dependencies]].

months" ([[2025-10-22 First call with OES - transcript]] 0:57:09); presale's iteration 3 made a start
