# COM-018 Written minutes, notices and amendments

## What is owed

Binds both parties; it matters most to us, since nothing agreed in a meeting counts until written.

- "No email, meeting note or proposal shall amend this Agreement unless expressly signed as an
  amendment by both Parties." (1.3); "No amendment, waiver or addition is effective unless made in
  writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties." (15.2)
- "Project meetings shall be conducted remotely unless the First Party determines otherwise. Meeting
  minutes become binding only when confirmed in writing by authorized representatives of both Parties
  and may not amend this Agreement unless they expressly state that they are a contractual amendment."
  (13.2)
- "Formal notices shall be in writing and sent by email to the addresses stated in this Agreement,
  with delivery capable of being evidenced. Routine operational communications may also be conducted
  through agreed electronic channels." (13.3)

([[2026-09-07 Final contract - rendering]])

The SOW v10.4: specifications and requirements "will be discussed and agreed upon through the
Contractor`s automated project management system and/or via email"
([[2026-06-15 SOW v10.4 - rendering]], assumptions). What Innowise told the client in December 2025:
"Requirements are approved in writing, either via email or using a project management tool"
([[2025-12-30 Answers to the client's questions - rendering]], 3).

## By when

Each time.

## How it is accepted

Written confirmation by authorised representatives of both parties. Who they are on the client side is
not named in the contract (5.1 says the client "nominate authorized contacts").

## Drives

- A demo or a nod is not acceptance (AGENTS.md rule 10) - the contract says the same.
