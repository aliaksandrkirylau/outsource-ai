# COM-010 Qualified personnel and approved subcontractors

## What is owed

"The Second Party shall perform the Services through qualified personnel and remains fully liable for
all personnel and approved subcontractors." "Subcontracting or assignment of any material part of the
Services requires the First Party's prior written approval. Approval shall not relieve the Second
Party of responsibility." ([[2026-09-07 Final contract - rendering]] 6.1, 6.2)

The SOW v10.4 adds a right: "The Contractor shall have the right, at its sole discretion, to replace a
specialist with a qualified equivalent, provided that the Contractor notifies the Customer in writing
at least fifteen (15) days in advance. The transfer of knowledge to the replacement specialist shall be
conducted by the Contractor and at the Contractor's expense." ([[2026-06-15 SOW v10.4 - rendering]] row
37)

Replacing a specialist and subcontracting are different things (added 2026-09-28, a query). 6.2
covers handing a material part of the Services to another company, or assigning the contract; it says
nothing about which of Innowise's own people do the work, and the contract has no clause on replacing
them. The two meet only where a team member is not employed by INNOWISE Sp. z o.o.: a freelancer, or
staff of another Innowise group entity. Whether staff of other Innowise group entities count as
subcontractors is not answered in these sources, and which entity employs whom is not recorded; see
also [[QUE-009 Where may our team members be located]]. IP assignments are required from "employees and
approved subcontractors" (8.2).

No named specialists, roles or headcount are committed, in the contract or in the SOW v10.4 (checked
2026-09-28). The replacement clause is the SOW's only mention of specialists. It came from PMO's
time-and-materials protections of 15 May 2026: "право Contractor заменять специалистов на equivalent
resources с предварительным уведомлением заказчика" (Yauheni Krasner,
[[2025-11-25 Contract chat - rendering 2]] 2026-05-15 07:04), a week before the switch to fixed price
([[DEC-008 Fixed price with milestones, on the client's contract]]). Two protections from the same list
are not in the SOW v10.4: "30-дневный notice period для снятия специалистов со стороны заказчика" and
the protection against sudden roll-off. Under fixed price the contract pays for completed work, not
people: "staffing allocation ... shall not by itself create any entitlement to payment" (7.5), and "Lack
of personnel" is not force majeure (12.4). So the clause does little now (evaluated).

  ([[2025-11-03 Solution proposal - rendering]], Team composition).

The contract names the Agreement, the SOW, the approved Financial Plan and signed Change Requests as
the entire agreement, superseding "prior proposals or understandings to the extent of inconsistency"
(15.1). Legal reading: ask a PM.

Inside, the team is not fixed yet: "определить финальный состав команды" (Yauheni Krasner's summary of
the 7 September call, point 7, [[2025-11-25 Contract chat - rendering 3]] 2026-09-07 10:32).
