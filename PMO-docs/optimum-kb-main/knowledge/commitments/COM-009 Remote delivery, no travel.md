# COM-009 Remote delivery, no travel

## What is owed

"All Services shall be performed remotely from the Second Party's own premises using its own
personnel, equipment and infrastructure. No on-site visit, physical inspection, travel, accommodation,
transportation or presence at the First Party's premises is required for the agreed scope."
([[2026-09-07 Final contract - rendering]] 5.2). The client bears no travel or similar expense of
Innowise or any third party, and "No future or final visit shall be required as a condition to
perform, complete, test, accept or deliver the Project." (5.4)

The SOW v10.4: "The quoted fees do not encompass expenses related to overseas travel or special
hardware or software licenses that may be required during the development period."
([[2026-06-15 SOW v10.4 - rendering]] row 36) - so any travel is Innowise's cost.

## By when

Throughout.

## How it is accepted

Not applicable.

How it came about (added 2026-09-28, batch 6; [[2025-11-25 Contract chat - rendering 3]]): the client,
7 July 2026: "all provisions relating to travel, accommodation, transportation, and any related expenses
should be removed entirely from the Agreement", since Innowise had received all project information
(2026-07-07 13:13). Innowise's letter of 14 July: "travel is not required under the Agreement", and if it
"exceptionally become necessary", terms and costs by a separate written agreement made in advance
(2026-07-14 15:18).
