# COM-007 Client answers clarifications within three business days

## What is owed

"The First Party shall respond to reasonable written clarification requests within three (3) business
days." ([[2026-09-07 Final contract - rendering]] 2.6)

## By when

Three business days per written request.

## How it is accepted

If the client misses it and the unanswered item makes a task "objectively impossible", Innowise may
suspend that task only, "after a clear written request" (4.5).

How it came about (added 2026-09-28, batch 6; [[2025-11-25 Contract chat - rendering 3]]): the earlier
text had 24 hours; the client, 7 July 2026: "The contractual obligation requiring responses within 24
hours is not practical for a project of this scale ... we propose replacing the current response period
with three (3) business days" (2026-07-07 13:13). Innowise agreed and asked "that the relevant project
activities and timelines may be suspended while we are awaiting the required information, approvals, or
decisions from your side" (2026-07-14 15:18). The client's final: three business days, but a late answer
does not extend the project; only a task that objectively cannot go on may pause (Yauheni Krasner's
review, 2026-08-13 09:26).
