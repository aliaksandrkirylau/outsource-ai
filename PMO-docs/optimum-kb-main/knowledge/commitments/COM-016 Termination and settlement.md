# COM-016 Termination and settlement

## What is owed

Binds both parties; party is set to client because the contract gives the rights to the client.
Disputed: the contract and the SOW v10.4 give different termination rights. Both kept.

### Version A - the contract

- The client may terminate for material breach not cured "within fifteen (15) business days after
  written notice, or immediately" for breaches incapable of cure, confidentiality or security, IP
  infringement, fraud, abandonment or "repeated material delay" (11.1), or "for convenience upon ten
  (10) business days' written notice" (11.2).
- Innowise "may terminate only for a material and undisputed payment default that continues for twenty
  (20) business days after written notice", not for clarification requests, disagreements or in-scope
  matters (11.3).
- On termination Innowise gets only "the value of work actually completed, delivered and objectively
  capable of being used", less amounts paid and the cost of remedying its breach (11.4); a disputed
  value goes to an independent technical expert appointed within ten business days (11.5, 11.6); all
  work, code, repositories and credentials are handed over with transition assistance (11.7).

([[2026-09-07 Final contract - rendering]])

### Version B - the SOW v10.4

"Either Party may terminate this SOW for convenience, and without other reason, upon providing written
notice at least thirty (30) days in advance of the expected date of termination."
([[2026-06-15 SOW v10.4 - rendering]] row 37)

### How the contract wording came about

From the contract chat (added 2026-09-28, batch 6); the dispute stays for Anton.

- June 2026: Innowise added to section 2 a refund of a share of the price
  ([[2025-11-25 Contract chat - rendering 2]] 2026-06-11 09:47; the share is removed).
- The client, 7 July: "neither party becomes automatically entitled to a predetermined [share removed]
  payment or refund upon termination. Instead, in the event of lawful termination, compensation should be
  limited to the actual value of the work completed up to the effective termination date", with an
  independent technical expert if the completion is disputed
  ([[2025-11-25 Contract chat - rendering 3]] 2026-07-07 13:13).
- Agreed inside: the expert mutually agreed, "процедура привлечения эксперта, его оплата и детали будут
  оформляться отдельным дополнительным соглашением" (2026-07-09 09:20); sent in Innowise's letter of 14
  July (15:18).
- The client's final contract: termination for convenience on ten business days for the client; for
  Innowise, only "material and undisputed payment default, продолжающемся 20 business days после notice.
  Других причин на расторжения у нас нету"; only completed, delivered and usable work paid (Yauheni
  Krasner, 2026-08-13 09:26). Innowise's request for mutual rights (2026-08-25 07:27) was declined
  (2026-09-02 15:55).
- Accepted as a known risk on 2026-09-07: "отсутствие возможности расторгнуть договор с нашей стороны,
  кроме payment default" (2026-09-07 10:32; [[DEC-009 Sign the client's final contract as it stands]]).
