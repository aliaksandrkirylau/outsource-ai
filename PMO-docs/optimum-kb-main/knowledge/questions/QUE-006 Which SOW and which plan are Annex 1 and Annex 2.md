# QUE-006 Which SOW and which plan are Annex 1 and Annex 2

## Question

Which document is the contract's "Annex 1 – Statement of Work (SOW)", and which is "Annex 2 – Approved
Financial Plan and Milestones"? Asks Sales and Legal first, then the client.

## What it blocks

The scope baseline: every Requirement marked contracted on the strength of the SOW v10.4, and
[[COM-012 Payment schedule]] and [[COM-020 Milestone plan and deliverables]].

## Options

- Annex 1 is the SOW v10.4 as it stands, with its cross-references to another contract version.
- Annex 1 is the SOW v10.4 brought in line with the final contract (numbering, registration number,
  clause references, fee schedule).
- Annex 2 is Article 7's schedule, the March 2026 plan, or a new plan.
