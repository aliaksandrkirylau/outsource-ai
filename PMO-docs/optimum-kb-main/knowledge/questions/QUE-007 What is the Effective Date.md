# QUE-007 What is the Effective Date

## Question

From which date does the contract's six-month delivery term run? Asks Sales and Legal first, then the
client.

## What it blocks

The due date of [[COM-001 Delivery within six months]], the month numbers of [[COM-012 Payment schedule]],
and the start of [[COM-020 Milestone plan and deliverables]].

## Options

- The date the client signs (the contract is not signed by the client yet).
- A start date agreed by e-mail, as the SOW v10.4 has it.
- A date written into the contract or its annexes.

## Answer

None. The contract uses "the Effective Date" (2.2, 2.4, 8.5, 10.3) and never defines it; the client's
signature and date are blank on the signed copy ([[2026-09-07 Final contract - overview]]). The SOW
v10.4: "The start date for the provision of services shall be agreed upon by the Parties via email."
([[2026-06-15 SOW v10.4 - rendering]] row 14)

Where signing stands (added 2026-09-28, batch 6). The client asked Innowise to sign first: print the
Agreement on Innowise letterhead, sign and stamp it, have it attested by the Chamber of Commerce and
legalised by Poland's Ministry of Foreign Affairs; then the client signs, attests and returns it
([[2025-11-25 Contract chat - rendering 3]] 2026-09-07 08:46). The legalisation "займет 1,5 недели"
(2026-09-17 12:49); "Договор с апостилем готов" (2026-09-28 08:20); the client's CEO is to collect the
original in the Warsaw office: "завтра их СЕО будет в нашем варшавском офисе в 11сет" (2026-09-28
12:37). So the client's signature, and any date it carries, is still ahead.
