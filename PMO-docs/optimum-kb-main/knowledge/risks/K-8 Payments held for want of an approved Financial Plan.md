# K-8 Payments held for want of an approved Financial Plan

## Risk

Cause: the contract pays "only to the Second Party's bank account registered in its legal name and
identified in the approved Financial Plan" ([[2026-09-07 Final contract - rendering]] 7.11) and names
Annex 2, "Approved Financial Plan and Milestones", but no such annex exists: the Agreement's Article 7
plan "shall be incorporated into and form an integral part of the Statement of Work (SOW)" (7.2), and the
stored SOW names no account ([[QUE-006 Which SOW and which plan are Annex 1 and Annex 2]]). Event: the
client holds an invoice, the advance first, until a Financial Plan is approved. Effect: work goes on
without payment.

## Why we think so

- Kavalchuk: "В контракте так же написано о Financial Plan - у нас оно есть? оно тоже должно быть
  подписано", "Там у нас мисэлаймент формулировок" ([[2025-11-25 Contract chat - rendering 4]]
  2026-10-12 12:54, 12:55); "Это надо будет одним из первых писем зафиксировать с ответственным лицом"
  (13:00).
- Our reading of 7.2 and 7.11 together; not checked with Legal.
- 14 October: "Так же они сделали запрос на оплату" (Kavalchuk, [[2026-10-13 OE Big4 chat - rendering 1]] 2026-10-14 08:25); his draft reply
  in the client's "Contract Review" thread: "Regarding the advance payment invoice and our bank details,
  Lizaveta will share them with you in this thread." (11:33). Kavalchuk had taken the payment on 13
  October: "Пеймент - разберусь - сделаю, сюда накину драфт перед высылкой клиенту" (09:51).
- On the Financial Plan itself, Mikhail proposes not raising it with the client, and the record of 13
  October leaves it to the PMs during discovery ([[QUE-006 Which SOW and which plan are Annex 1 and Annex 2]]).
