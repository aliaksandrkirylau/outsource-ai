# K-6 No suitable BA in time

## Risk

Cause: the plan needs a senior BA who works at product level and builds prototypes with Claude
([[DEC-014 Staff on demand, seniors who work with Claude, fewer developers]]), and such people are rare.
Event: no suitable BA within one to two weeks. Effect: discovery starts late, or with a weaker BA and
slower prototypes, while the term may already be running ([[K-3 The six months run from the client's signature, before discovery starts]]).

  сможет" (Mikhail, [[2026-10-13 OE Big4 chat - rendering 1]] 2026-10-13 09:20).

  его найдем" (Mikhail, 2026-10-14 11:39).
