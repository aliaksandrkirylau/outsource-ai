# K-3 The six months run from the client's signature, before discovery starts

## Risk

Cause: the contract runs the delivery term "six (6) months from the Effective Date" and never
defines it ([[2026-09-07 Final contract - rendering]] 2.2; [[QUE-007 What is the Effective Date]]). Event:
the client counts the six months from its signature of 7 October 2026. Effect: getting a client contact,
hiring the BA and the whole discovery eat into a term that is already running, with no relief for the
client's own delays ([[COM-001 Delivery within six months]], [[COM-008 Client cooperation and dependencies]]).

## Why we think so

- Aliaksandra Kalevich-Palonskaya, who prepared the legalisation, 29 September: "В договоре явно не
  определен срок его вступления в силу, соответственно Договор вступает в силу с даты подписания
  последней из сторон" ([[2025-11-25 Contract chat - rendering 4]] 2026-09-29 12:27).
- "Контракт подписан 07.10.2026" and "По поводу начала от даты подписания контракта срок пошёл"
  (Kavalchuk, 2026-10-12 12:22).
- Mikhail: "Теоретически клиент может сказать, ну вот дата подписания, это и есть effective date, но
  это как бы так, за уши притянуто" ([[2026-10-13 Optimum Three Caballeros call - transcript]] 00:06:30).
- Our reading: the readings above disagree, and the client is the one who decides how to count.
