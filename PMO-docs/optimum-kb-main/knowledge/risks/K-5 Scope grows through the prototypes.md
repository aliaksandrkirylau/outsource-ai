# K-5 Scope grows through the prototypes

## Risk

Cause: a fixed price with acceptance repeated until the client says yes
([[COM-002 Written acceptance of each deliverable]]), and a BA who designs beyond what each WBS line holds.
Event: the client sees more in the prototypes than the WBS line was estimated for, and asks for it.
Effect: hours many times the estimate, with no change request the client will sign.

## Why we think so

- Mikhail: "Это fixed price под очень туманные acceptance критерии. по которым клиент может просить
  нас переделывать по 5, 10, 20, 100 раз" ([[2026-10-13 Optimum Three Caballeros call - transcript]]
  00:25:22).
- Kavalchuk: "Вот если мы арабам нафантазируем космолет неосознанно, то потом они это увидят и скажут,
  делайте" (00:31:29); the fear that the analyst comes back with "ну, ребята, бюджет x5" (00:42:44).
