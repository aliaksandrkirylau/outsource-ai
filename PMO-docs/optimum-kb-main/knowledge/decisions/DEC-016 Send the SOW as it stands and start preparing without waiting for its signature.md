# DEC-016 Send the SOW as it stands and start preparing without waiting for its signature

## Context

The client signed the Agreement on 7 October ([[2025-11-25 Contract chat - rendering 4]] 2026-10-12
10:01, 12:22); the SOW, Annex 1, is not signed
([[QUE-006 Which SOW and which plan are Annex 1 and Annex 2]]). The SOW had been brought in line with the
Agreement, reviewed by PMO and Legal by 5 October, and its changed Arabic text translated by 9 October.
Whether the six months have started is open ([[QUE-007 What is the Effective Date]]).

## Options considered

1. Hold all work until the SOW is signed. Kavalchuk, 29 September: "пока стартовать нечего, нам нужно
   довыслать SOW и подписать его, прежде чем мы начнём какие-либо работы" (2026-09-29 12:25); Mikhail,
   12 October: "обычно SOW является контрактом на определенный скоуп работ и его подписание является
   поводом к старту" (2026-10-12 12:51). Not taken: "Контракт подписан, я не знаю, какое решение мы
   ждём" (Kavalchuk, 13:01).
2. Hold the SOW and rewrite its scope as we think we can deliver it (Kavalchuk,
   [[2026-10-13 Optimum Three Caballeros call - transcript]] 00:22:39). Not taken: the client would not
   accept changes to its statements - "Не сможем стейтменты менять" (00:31:29) - and Mikhail: "Мы уже
   подставились, подписав framework agreement" (00:22:21).
3. Send the SOW as it stands, refine the scope by letter after discovery, and start preparing now.
   Chosen.

## Decision

"сов, нет смысла сейчас тормозить, надо высылать, как есть" (Kavalchuk, 00:31:29); "После discovery мы
пишем письмо и говорим, клиент дорогой, с изменениями после дискавери, вот от сова есть уточнение"
(00:22:39). Preparation starts now, alongside: the BA search and the discovery plan (Anton: "Я займусь
сетапом и поском БА в параллели", [[2025-11-25 Contract chat - rendering 4]] 2026-10-12 13:04;
[[DEC-015 Discovery - our own picture of the system, questions as if from scratch]]). No start date is
named to the client: "я в любом случае не говорю о каких-то сроках, когда мы начали" (Kavalchuk,
00:20:39).

Still open: whether we start before the client pays the advance - "Вопрос, возможно, к лидершику будет,
а стартанем ли мы, например, без prepayment" (Anton, 00:02:02).

Not named as decided by anyone; deciders tbd (Anton Oparienko, ingest session 2026-10-13).

Since 13 October (added 2026-10-14): the published record of the call: "Текущую версию SOW не меняем и

## Rationale

A week or two of holding the SOW would not clarify the scope (Mikhail, "Нет, мы не сможем
разобраться", 00:26:59), and the client's answers are slow, so the contact request should go out now
(Kavalchuk, 00:29:33).

## Consequences

- Downside: whatever scope the SOW fixes binds acceptance - "ты по факту юридически будешь их
  переделывать ровно столько раз, пока клиент тебе не скажет «да, хорошо»" (Kavalchuk, 00:24:33);
  [[COM-002 Written acceptance of each deliverable]], [[K-5 Scope grows through the prototypes]].
- Downside: work and spending start before the SOW and the advance; see
  [[K-8 Payments held for want of an approved Financial Plan]].
- New obligation: store the SOW as sent, and re-check the five disputed Commitments against it.
- New obligation: the Annex 2 wording settled in one of the first letters to the client's responsible
  person (Kavalchuk, 2026-10-12 13:00).
  No longer agreed as written (added 2026-10-14): Mikhail proposes leaving Annex 2 unraised with the
  client, "спустить этот момент на тормозах", and the published record leaves it to Mikhail, Anton and
  Arseniy "в ходе Discovery" ([[QUE-006 Which SOW and which plan are Annex 1 and Annex 2]]).
- Against [[DEC-009 Sign the client's final contract as it stands]]: consistent, the same line on the
  Agreement.
